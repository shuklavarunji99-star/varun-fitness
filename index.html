DIET & NUTRITION

Simple habits that can support your fitness goals.

💪 Muscle Building

Eat sufficient calories and protein. Include dal, beans, milk, curd, soy, paneer or other suitable protein foods.

🔥 Weight Management

Build balanced meals with vegetables, protein and high-fibre foods. Aim for sustainable habits rather than crash diets.

💧 Hydration & Recovery

Drink water regularly, sleep enough and allow your muscles time to recover between hard sessions.

Nutrition information is general education, not personalised medical advice.
